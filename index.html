Executive Context

The Commercial Problem & Core Inquiries

Global Retail Corp achieved record revenue across 4 US regions, yet operational profit margin remained capped at 12.49%, falling short of the 15.00% executive target.

How have revenue and profit margins trended month-over-month, and what seasonality curves emerge during Q4 holiday shopping? Historical data reveals dramatic November/December surges averaging over $330,000 monthly, with consistent positive cash flows across all 46 operating months.
Which product lines generate cash vs. which operate as chronic loss-makers? Technology Copiers generates $1.14M in net profit (33.9% margin), whereas Furniture Tables (-$296.1K) and Bookcases (-$76.7K) suffer severe negative margins due to excessive promotional discounting combined with bulky freight shipping costs.
At what exact discount rate does transactional margin turn negative? Undiscounted orders achieve 22.98% margin. Margins decline to 11.28% (low tier) and 4.68% (moderate tier), then collapse into steep negative losses (-8.91% and -38.53%) when discounts exceed 20%.
Which regions yield the highest volume versus highest profit efficiency? The West leads volume ($3.18M, 33.1% share), but the Central region delivers peak operating margin efficiency (14.23%, $413.2K profit), anchored by high-performing corporate hubs in Texas and Michigan.
How do Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments compare? Consumer accounts for 52.3% of volume ($5.03M). However, Home Office accounts achieve both the highest ticket size ($1,005.23 AOV) and the highest net margin (13.50%), representing prime targets for specialized VIP loyalty programs.
What actionable commercial policies will recover lost profit? Enforcing a 20% discount ceiling (+ $253.96K), adding a $35–$60 freight surcharge on heavy furniture (+ $95K), and scaling enterprise copier fleet leasing (+ $180K) generates an estimated + $638.96K in annualized profit expansion (+ 53.2% increase).

Automated Cleaning & Feature Engineering (src/data_cleaning.py)

Ingests raw CSV data, deduplicates records, resolves missing postal codes via geographical state-city lookups, standardizes mixed-case strings, and computes essential business features.

src/data_cleaning.py (Core Transformation Logic) 
# 1. Currency & Numeric Cleaning
df["Sales"] = df["Sales"].apply(clean_currency).round(2)
df["Profit"] = df["Profit"].apply(clean_currency).round(2)
df["Quantity"] = pd.to_numeric(df["Quantity"], errors="coerce").fillna(1).astype(int)
df["Discount"] = pd.to_numeric(df["Discount"], errors="coerce").fillna(0.0).round(2)

# 2. Impute Missing Postal Codes via State-City Lookup
geo_lookup = df[~df["Postal_Code"].isna()][["State", "City", "Postal_Code"]].drop_duplicates(subset=["State", "City"]).set_index(["State", "City"])["Postal_Code"].to_dict()
df["Postal_Code"] = df.apply(lambda r: geo_lookup.get((r["State"], r["City"]), "90001") if pd.isna(r["Postal_Code"]) else str(r["Postal_Code"])[:5], axis=1)

# 3. Feature Engineering: Margins, Unit Prices, and Calendar Dimensions
df["Profit_Margin"] = np.where(df["Sales"] > 0, (df["Profit"] / df["Sales"]).round(4), 0.0)
df["Unit_Price"] = (df["Sales"] / (df["Quantity"] * (1.0 - df["Discount"]).replace(0.0, 0.01))).round(2)
df["Order_Year"] = df["Order_Date"].dt.year
df["Order_Month_Year"] = df["Order_Date"].dt.strftime("%Y-%m")
df["Order_to_Ship_Days"] = (df["Ship_Date"] - df["Order_Date"]).dt.days

# 4. Segment into Elasticity Discount Tiers
bins = [-0.01, 0.00, 0.10, 0.20, 0.40, 1.00]
labels = ["0% None", "1-10% Low", "11-20% Moderate", "21-40% High", ">40% Deep"]
df["Discount_Tier"] = pd.cut(df["Discount"], bins=bins, labels=labels)

Advanced SQL Window Functions (sql/02_revenue_profit_trends.sql)

Calculates Month-over-Month (MoM) revenue growth using LAG(), a 3-month trailing moving average using ROWS BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW, and running cumulative sales totals.

sql/02_revenue_profit_trends.sql 
WITH monthly_metrics AS (
    SELECT 
        Order_Month_Year,
        COUNT(DISTINCT Order_ID) AS Total_Orders,
        ROUND(SUM(Sales), 2) AS Monthly_Sales,
        ROUND(SUM(Profit), 2) AS Monthly_Profit,
        ROUND((SUM(Profit) / SUM(Sales)) * 100.0, 2) AS Profit_Margin_Pct
    FROM retail_sales
    GROUP BY Order_Month_Year
)
SELECT 
    Order_Month_Year,
    Total_Orders,
    Monthly_Sales,
    Monthly_Profit,
    Profit_Margin_Pct,
    ROUND(((Monthly_Sales - LAG(Monthly_Sales, 1) OVER (ORDER BY Order_Month_Year)) 
          / LAG(Monthly_Sales, 1) OVER (ORDER BY Order_Month_Year)) * 100.0, 2) AS MoM_Sales_Growth_Pct,
    ROUND(AVG(Monthly_Sales) OVER (ORDER BY Order_Month_Year ROWS BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW), 2) AS Rolling_3M_Avg_Sales,
    ROUND(SUM(Monthly_Sales) OVER (ORDER BY Order_Month_Year), 2) AS Cumulative_Sales
FROM monthly_metrics
ORDER BY Order_Month_Year;

power_bi/dax_measures.dax (Selected DAX Measures) 
// Core KPIs
[Total Sales] = SUM(fact_sales[Sales])
[Total Profit] = SUM(fact_sales[Profit])
[Profit Margin %] = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)
[Average Order Value] = DIVIDE([Total Sales], [Total Orders], 0)

// Time Intelligence
[Sales YTD] = TOTALYTD([Total Sales], dim_date[Date_Key])
[Sales Prior Year (PY)] = CALCULATE([Total Sales], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(dim_date[Date_Key]))
[YoY Sales Growth %] = DIVIDE([Total Sales] - [Sales Prior Year (PY)], [Sales Prior Year (PY)], 0)
[Sales 3M Rolling Avg] = CALCULATE(AVERAGEX(DATESINPERIOD(dim_date[Date_Key], LASTDATE(dim_date[Date_Key]), -3, MONTH), [Total Sales]))

// Loss Bleed & EBITDA Reclamation
[Unprofitable Lines Count] = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fact_sales), fact_sales[Profit] < 0)
[Gross Margin Bleed] = CALCULATE(SUM(fact_sales[Profit]), fact_sales[Profit] < 0)

Executive Strategy

Quantified EBITDA Upside & 180-Day Roadmap

Executing these four targeted interventions unlocks an estimated +$638,959 in annual profit (+53.2% increase), expanding corporate operating margin from 12.49% to 17.99%.

Phase 1 (Days 0–30)

Immediate Triage & Policy Caps

  • Hard ERP Discount Ceiling: Program automated system restriction capping discounts at 20% max.
  • Furniture Cap @ 10%: Require VP approval for discounts > 10% on Tables and Bookcases.
  • Daily Loss Monitor: Automated alert triggered whenever any order line logs Profit < 0.
  • Impact: Reclaims +$253,959 in EBITDA.
Phase 2 (Days 31–90)

Logistics & Product Bundling

  • Heavy Freight Surcharge: Implement $35–$60 pass-through shipping surcharge on heavy furniture.
  • High-Margin Bundling: Pair loss-making desks with high-margin tech accessories (Logitech mouse @ 42% margin).
  • Home Office Loyalty Tier: Target the 119 top accounts with customized corporate hardware bundles.
  • Impact: Reclaims +$95,000 in EBITDA.
Phase 3 (Days 91–180)

Fleet Expansion & Growth

  • Copier Fleet Contracts: Scale 3-year recurring toner and service contracts on Canon & HP copiers.
  • Regional Headcount Rebalance: Deploy dedicated commercial B2B sales reps to Texas, California, and Michigan hubs.
  • Inventory Hub Optimization: Establish Dallas & LA staging hubs to reduce LTL transit delays.
  • Impact: Expands profit by +$290,000 in EBITDA.
